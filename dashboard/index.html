How the model works

One formula scores every row, from the snapshot’s own figures only. No protocol name is ever special-cased.

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains

  • Category multiple. The median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live-token protocols in the same category, where the category has at least three live comparables — otherwise the whole market’s median — shrunk toward that market median. A DEX token is worth a different share of its TVL than a lending token, and the snapshot says by how much. One protocol’s ratio never prices a whole category.
  • Growth. 1 + the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50% … +100%, at half weight. Fast-growing TVL is worth more; a shrinking one less.
  • Maturity. 0.70 at launch, rising to 1.00 after three years with no token. A protocol that has run longer has a bigger base to reward, but the effect saturates.
  • Chains. +5% per chain beyond the first, up to five extra chains. Multi-chain reach is wider distribution for a launch.

What the radar does not claim

  • “Records no token” is all the snapshot proves. A protocol on the board may still hold a token its DefiLlama record does not carry.
  • Implied value is what a token would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is. It says nothing about whether or when a token is issued.
  • The snapshot is sealed at its fetch date; nothing here updates it. A farm plan is a starting point, not financial advice.
